# Flame in Freefall Dashboard Dashboard that helps users find, rank and interpret NASA microgravity combustion investigations for the fire safety of crewed environments (space station, lunar base, pressurized rover). Built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge "Flame in Freefall" (Casablanca local event). ## Live demo https://claude.ai/artifact/9EFZ7wLQJwgQLZdka59JvY ## Modules 1. Investigation Explorer: ranks NASA PSI combustion investigations by relevance to a chosen crew environment 2. Flammability Lab: relative fire risk index for solid materials as a function of oxygen and pressure, with a flame illustration by gravity 3. AI Insights: plain-language summaries traced to PSI sources (in progress) 4. Earth Applications: terrestrial fire safety links named in the challenge ## Data sources - NASA Physical Science Informatics (PSI): https://psi.nasa.gov - NASA PSI research opportunity page (list of combustion investigations) ## Status Prototype. Relevance scores and the flammability model are team heuristics based on literature values at 1 g, and are being checked against PSI data. Not an operational safety system. ## Tools and AI use Interface code drafted with Gemini. Dashboard logic drafted with Claude. All content is reviewed by the team. ## Team (CHEMPHILES) ## License MIT
