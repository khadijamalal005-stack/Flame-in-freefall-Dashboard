# Flame in Freefall Dashboard

Dashboard that helps users find, rank and interpret NASA microgravity
combustion investigations for the fire safety of crewed environments
(space station, lunar base, pressurized rover).

Built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge "Flame in Freefall"
(Casablanca local event).

## Modules
1. Investigation explorer and ranking by crew environment
2. AI interpretation of investigations (in progress)
3. Flammability predictor for solid materials (in progress)

## Data sources
- NASA Physical Science Informatics (PSI): https://psi.nasa.gov
- NASA PSI research opportunity page (list of combustion investigations)

## Status
Prototype. Relevance scores are team heuristics and are being checked
against the PSI investigation pages.
