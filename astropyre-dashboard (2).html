4. Earth Applications

The challenge asks how PSI combustion research can improve fire safety on Earth. Examples named in the challenge statement:

Fine water mist extinguishers

Developed for use on the ISS, with practical applications on Earth. Related investigations: FLEX, FLEX-2 (flame extinguishment).

Smoke detection

NASA technology has improved terrestrial smoke detection. Related investigations: SAME, SAME-R, DAFT, SPICE.

Firefighter gear

Protective clothing and air tanks benefited from NASA technology. Material flammability studies: BASS, BASS-II, SAFFIRE.
